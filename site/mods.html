NICKI NIGHT · Staff Guide

Everything you need to help run the night. Short and simple.

Getting in

1
Open the link you were sent on your phone or tablet.
2
Type your PIN. Your PIN is not case-sensitive.
3
Tap your name. It shows on stream when you press a button, so everyone sees who did what. You only do this once on each device.

The dot at the top turns green when you are connected. Buttons do nothing until it is green.

Hosts and DJ

Tap a button and it happens on stream right away. Buttons that are lit up are on.

Lights
Chill, Party, The drop, or Lights off. Tap The drop when the DJ drops a big song.
Drink menu
Shows the drink specials board for 20 seconds. Tap again to close it. Say it on the mic first.
Round on the house
Everyone at the bar gets a drink with a cheers animation.
Top 5 stage
Puts the five most active people on the stage. Tap again to end it.
Runway
Starts the fashion contest. Contestants walk, chat votes by typing 1 to 5, and the winner is announced. Tap again to stop it early.
End event
Ends a runway, Top 5 or an entrance right away and puts everyone back where they were. Events also do this by themselves when they finish.
Shout
Type a short message like HAPPY BIRTHDAY KAY and tap Show. It appears in big text across the club.
Tell chat
Lines you can say or paste in chat so people know what to do.

Mods

Your job is to keep the club safe and fun.

Find someone
Type part of their name. Everyone inside shows up in the list.
Hide photo
For a bad profile picture. The avatar stays and the face is hidden. You can show it again.
Block
For bad behavior. Tap twice to confirm. They leave the club and their messages are ignored.
Emergency
If chat is flooded with nasty messages, pause the chat bubbles. Turn them back on when it calms down.
End event
If a runway, Top 5 or entrance looks stuck, tap this. Everyone goes back to the party.
Add a VIP
Type their @ and tap Add. They are welcomed into the VIP lounge.
Undo
Lists everyone you blocked or hid. Tap to bring them back.

Not sure? Hide the photo first. It is the gentle option and easy to undo.

Chat commands

You can also type these in the live chat.

!vip @name
Add to the VIP list. !unvip @name removes them.
!light party
Change the lights: chill, party, drop, dark, off.
!top5 on
Top 5 stage. Use off to end it.
!runway 4
Start a runway with 4 guests. Use off to stop.
!np Artist - Song
Update Now Playing. !np off clears it.

Anyone in the club can type !enter to walk in, or paste their look from the Style Studio. They can try !dance twerk, !cheers and !starships once inside.

Show flow

Doors open
Welcome everyone. Share the Style Studio link and tell people to paste their look in chat.
First 20 minutes
Lights on Chill. Switch to Party when the DJ gets going.
Mid show
Drink menu, a round on the house, Top 5, and a Runway.
Big moments
Lights to Party and tap The drop on the beat.
Shout-outs
Birthdays and VIP arrivals look great as a Shout.

If something looks off

Dot is not green
Wait a few seconds, then refresh the page. If it stays red, tell the producer.
"Switched off" message
The producer turned your panel off for now. It comes back when they turn it on.
Wrong name showing
Tap change at the top and pick yours.
Someone cannot wear something
Some items are unlocked by joining in on the night. Tell the producer if it looks wrong.
Anything else
Message the producer. Please do not share your panel link or PIN.
