VIRTUAL NICKI NIGHT

Your own animated character in the club. Your chat messages are your controls. No app, no download.

✨ Style your look now

✨ Get ready before the show

  1. Build your look in the Style Studio: top, bottoms, shoes, hair, extras, skin tone and body. Add your @ to see your profile pic on your character.
  2. Tap Copy my look. Save the line somewhere handy, like your notes.
  3. Come to the live on X or Kick. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to download.

🪩 Getting in

  1. Type !enter in the live chat. Your character walks in through the door.
  2. Paste your !look … line to change into your outfit. Change it as often as you like.
  3. Just chat! Your messages pop up in a bubble over your character.

💃 Commands

!enterWalk into the club
!look …Change your outfit (build it in the Style Studio)
!danceDance. Add a move: groove, bounce, rock, shuffle, pump, wave, bboy, twerk, snake

!drinkOrder a drink: martini, shot, beer, cocktail, champagne, wine

!code wordHeard a secret code? Type it here
!leaveHead home

Commands start with ! and go in the live chat. They never show up as chat bubbles.

🔓 Unlock more outfits

Some pieces wear a little lock 🔒. Each one unlocks by taking part during the live. The riddle on the lock is your hint. Some come quick, some take effort, and a few are secret, so listen for clues from the hosts.

Type !earned any time to see your progress and get a hint. Pieces with a 👑 are for the VIP list.

Everyone starts fresh each live. Wearing a locked piece shows its riddle, and the rest of your look still works.

💗 Good to know

  • Quiet for a while and you drift to the back. Chat and you're back up front.
  • When the club gets busy, characters get a little smaller so everyone fits.
  • Links, phone numbers and filtered words never show on screen.
  • Be kind. The hosts can remove anyone who isn't.
  • Lights can flash during drops. Take care if that bothers you.
