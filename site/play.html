Unlock more outfits

Some pieces show a lock 🔒. Each one has its own challenge you complete during the live. The riddle on the lock hints at what it takes. Some are quick, some take real effort, and a few are secret, so listen for hints from Nicki and the hosts. Everyone starts fresh every live.

Trying to wear a locked piece shows its riddle. The rest of your command still works.

What you will see

  • Dance floor and bars: that is you and everyone else. Quiet for about 5 minutes and you drift to the back. Chat and you are back up front.
  • Top 5: the five biggest chatters step onto the center stage with gold, silver and bronze medals.
  • Runway: a few guests model on stage. Vote for best dressed by typing their number.
  • VIP lounge: upper right, for the host's VIP list.
  • Last call: at the end everyone heads out and a thank-you card shows the night's highlights.

Good to know

  • If you are not on the dance floor, your picture may be a letter. Some platforms (Kick) do not share profile pics.
  • Links, phone numbers and filtered words never show as bubbles.
  • Be kind. The hosts can remove anyone who is not.
  • Lights can flash during drops. The strobe is limited, but take care if that bothers you.
