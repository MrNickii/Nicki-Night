HOST & MOD GUIDE

How to run Virtual Nicki Night: the control panel, chat commands, and show flow.

Before the show

  • OBS layers: the overlay is a Browser source at 1920x1080. Put your video sources where the screens are. Either put the video above the overlay (text is kept off the video windows), or tick See-through main screen in the overlay settings and put the video underneath so announcements show over it.
  • Control panel: open your control panel link on any phone or tablet. It asks for the PIN.
  • Check: open Connection and confirm chat is connected. Run the Feature checklist and Demo reel once to see everything work.
  • Lists: add VIP handles under VIP list and your hosts under Mods. Mods can use the chat commands below.
  • Set up: set the BPM to match the DJ (Tap works), write your closing message under Close the club, and set the Secret codes words.
  • New show: press New show in the Nicki card. It resets the top 5 counts, everyone's unlock progress and the recap numbers, and lets Nicki's entrance play again.

The control panel

Six tabs along the bottom: Show, Room, Crowd, Looks, Test, Setup. Each tab has a row of jump buttons at the top that scroll to a card. Tap a card title to fold or open it. Setup and Test start folded.

Panels

Three panels
Control (main operator, everything). Host panel (host.html): lights, drink menu, round of drinks, Top 5, runway, banners, Now Playing and a cheat sheet of what to tell chat. Mod panel (mod.html): search people, Hide photo, Block (tap twice), word filter, VIP list, emergency bubbles off, and undo. All use the same PIN. Copy the links from the Home tab of Control.
Home tab
The main Control panel opens on Home: moods, disco floor, lasers, blackout, drink menu, round of drinks, Top 5, runway, banner, Nicki, last call.
Drink specials
Starships Punch, Cybertruck Smash, Nicki's Strawberry Daq', Barbz on the Rocks. Viewers type !starships, !cybertruck, !daq, !barbz; each has its own pop-up. !menu shows the board (once every 45 seconds from chat). Trying one special or all four unlocks earned pieces.

Show moments

Nicki
Entrance and exit, outfit buttons, rehearse the entrance, and New show.
Shout to the room
Type a message and flash it as a big banner.
Runway
Pick how many guests (2 to 6) and the vote time. They walk on stage, chat votes by number, and the winner gets the spotlight and fireworks.
Top 5 stage
Puts the five biggest chatters on the center stage with medals.
Secret codes
Four code words, each unlocks one special piece. Edit the word, then press Reveal when you say it on stream. It also pops on screen. Hide closes it.
Close the club
Last call (15 to 60 seconds) with warm lights, everyone walks out, then a thank-you card (top dancers, chatters, best dressed, peak crowd, your message). Cancel reopens.

Lights tab

Right now
Shows the current mood, which effects are on, the wash and brightness, and warns you if Blackout or Performance mode is on.
Moods
One tap sets the whole room: Chill, Party, The drop, Dark + spot, Lights off. The active mood is lit up.
Effects
Each button shows ON or OFF. Beams, lasers, sparkles, beat pulse, strobe, fog, spotlight, blackout, glows and LED strips. Strobe is capped at 3 flashes per second.
Color wash
Tints the whole room.
Intensity & tempo
Brightness and BPM. The speakers and pulse follow the BPM.
Speakers
Speakers move on or off, and how hard.
Performance mode
For a slow computer: glows, LED strips and fog hold still, lights redraw at half speed with simpler blending, and avatar shadows hide. Beams, lasers and the other lights still work. It turns itself on if the overlay struggles for 8 seconds, and that resets when the overlay reloads.
City window
Window lights, traffic and fireworks on or off.
Chat bubbles
Show bubbles over characters. Quiet mode for big crowds.
Now playing
Set the artist and song on the bar under the screen.

People

Who is in
Counts for floor, bars and VIP.
People
Tap anyone to spotlight them, send them to the bar or floor, make them VIP, grant all unlocks, hide their photo, block, or remove. It also lists what they have unlocked.
Dress someone
Pick a look in the picker and apply it to a handle.
Whole room
Everyone dances one move, a round on the house at the bars.
Guests & free message
Add test guests and send chat messages as them.

Safety

Moderation
Blocked people and hidden photos. Blocked means removed and ignored.
Bubble word filter
Words that never show as bubbles. Links and phone numbers are always hidden.
VIP list / Mods
Handles by platform. VIPs walk into the lounge, mods can use chat commands.

Setup and tests

Unlock rules & limits
Dance and chat numbers for the classic unlock, and the room size limit.
Demo reel / Feature checklist

What the overlay has been doing. Useful if something looks off.

Suggested show flow

  • Before doors: New show, lights on chill, Now Playing set.
  • Doors open: post the Style Studio link. People type !enter.
  • First 20 minutes: welcome people, light party when the DJ gets going. Say a secret code word on stream and press Reveal.
  • Mid show: turn on Top 5 for a few minutes, run a Runway, do a round on the house.
  • Big moment: Nicki's entrance. Lights to party, drop on the beat.
  • End: Close the club. Last call, then the thank-you card with your message.

If something looks off

  • Chat says not connected: check the Connection card and that the chat source is live. The overlay reconnects by itself.
  • Overlay froze or you refreshed it: safe. The crowd and positions come back.
  • Someone is spamming or being rude: tap them in People, then Block for good.
  • Someone cannot wear something: they probably have not earned it. Tap them, then Unlock all.
  • Crowd looks small: that is the auto fit. Big crowds shrink the characters so everyone has room.
  • Random strangers appear: change the chat session ID in Social Stream Ninja, then update the link in OBS and your control panel.
  • Built-in protection: chat bursts are queued so a flood can never freeze the show, one person cannot spam more than 6 messages in 3 seconds, avatars animate at 30 frames per second (add &fps=60 to the overlay link for 60), and the overlay slows far-away characters on its own when the crowd is big.
  • Lag: turn on Performance mode in the Lights tab first. Then in OBS Browser source properties, set the FPS to 30, keep the shutdown-when-hidden option on, and close extra browser tabs on the streaming PC.
