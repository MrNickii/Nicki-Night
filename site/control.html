Chat bubbles

Each chat shows above that person's character, in their own color with their name, and they step forward and glow while it's up. Quiet club: everyone gets one and it stays longer. Busy club: shorter and smaller. Packed club: front half of the room only (VIPs and Nicki always show), newest first. A message that is only emoji shoots up from the character instead. Commands (like !dance) never make a bubble.
